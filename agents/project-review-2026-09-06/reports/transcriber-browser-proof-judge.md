### BPJ-01 [P1] Chromium redirect hops bypass the supposed pinned-redirect enforcement

- Location: [routing callback](C:/Users/chamb/OneDrive/Desktop/accessfreetools-browser-transcriber/scripts/transcriber-browser-compatibility.mjs:79), especially lines 81 and 87-89; [redirect classifier](C:/Users/chamb/OneDrive/Desktop/accessfreetools-browser-transcriber/scripts/lib/transcriber-browser-proof.mjs:37).

- Consequence/confidence: high-confidence false assurance that every request/redirect was checked. A disallowed or sensitive redirect can escape the counts, while `noUnexpectedNetwork` remains true. - Minimal fix/acceptance: observe every request event, including redirect ancestry, with sanitized identities; distinguish observation from actual prevention. If containment is claimed, enforce each hop with a mechanism that actually intercepts redirects, such as controlled CDP Fetch interception for page and dedicated-worker targets, or carefully bounded manual redirect handling. Add a model-free local redirect-chain test that proves both approved delivery and rejected off-policy/sentinel hops. Do not simply widen the Hugging Face allowlist.
