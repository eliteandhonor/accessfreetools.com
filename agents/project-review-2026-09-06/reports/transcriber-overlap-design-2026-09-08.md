# Transcriber Overlap Design: 2026-09-08

## Scoped Judgment

**Reject blind source-time/midpoint ownership as the correction. Bounded, contextual PCM re-recognition before the current outer block is checkpointed is contract-compatible, with an atomic, coverage-checked replacement or loss-preserving fallback.** The new isolated prototype supports this direction, but its acceptance predicate must not be ported unchanged. No product fix, TR-01 acceptance, or release is approved here.

Source bounds prove which audio each call received, not which lexical hypothesis is correct or how many occurrences were spoken. Ownership is defensible as bookkeeping, or as a tie-break after equivalent words have a unique positive-duration source-overlap match. Using it to discard a point word, an unmatched occurrence, or non-equivalent text violates the present preservation contract. Re-recognition can provide additional source-conditioned evidence; a monotonic, nonempty result alone cannot authorize replacement either.

## Minimal Proposed Rule

Owner: parent transcriber implementation lane, within existing TR-01. Keep the current ordinary merge and outer checkpoint projection unchanged. Preserve per-call source bounds and raw candidate words only in the local, uncommitted reconciliation scope; do not add transcript logging or persistent PCM.

1. **Select a bounded conflict.** Use actual intersecting source-aligned words from distinct overlapping recognition calls, including their attached point tails. Take the connected closure of whole affected captions and every other caption/word intersecting that closure. Endpoint points belong to the closure for preservation, never as positive duplicate evidence. Unknown alignment or ambiguous occurrence association means retain/review, not guess a precise repair target.
2. **Recognize actual context once.** Take the closure's source envelope plus 2.5 seconds of existing PCM on each side, following the isolated candidate. Require the entire crop to lie inside the current outer block and be <=480,000 actual samples; do not shrink the target, pad invented silence, or borrow completed-block state. The nominal crop for this numeric case is [148.22,158.86], 10.64 seconds, 170,240 samples at 16 kHz before floating-point rounding checks. Derive indices from the actual block sample origin; do not offset twice. Recheck full-caption closure on the quantized bounds. Initially allow at most one repair call per outer block, no recursive retry; additional unresolved conflicts remain flagged. This is a work-budget bound, not a smaller supported input limit.
3. **Validate observed alignment before considering replacement.** Use the existing builder's finite, monotonic, in-crop, text/chunk-consistency and positive-support checks. Reject fallback, empty, all-point, backwards, out-of-range, edge-truncated or still-conflicting candidates. Preserve supplied point times exactly. No timestamp clamping, midpoint word splitting, or artificial positive duration. A candidate may shift within the supplied context, but cannot collide with untouched neighboring captions.
4. **Prove coverage by ordered words, not by the enclosing timestamp range.** Match the replacement against complete original candidate sequences using the current conservative lexical normalization and unique source-overlapping positive anchors. Every stable word occurrence and every separate repetition must survive, in order; this includes text present in only one candidate. Do not accept a result that merely spans their times. Permit a lexical disagreement to resolve only as a uniquely located substitution between shared positive anchors, where the retry corroborates one complete original hypothesis. Do not assemble a preferred sentence from pieces of competing hypotheses. Novel alternatives, insertion/deletion ambiguity, an unanchored disagreement, incompatible CJK tokenization, or unmatched point text retain the originals with review. A truncated point tail may be associated through a complete ordered sequence corroborated by the other original's positive word and the retry; the point itself supplies no positive match. Ambiguous repetition still rejects the association.
5. **Replace atomically, only within the uncommitted region.** Keep original candidates until all checks pass. Splice whole covered captions using actual retry words/times. Re-recognized context must not be appended as new speech: match it uniquely to untouched context, or reject. If coverage requires another caption, include it only when it is wholly covered by the already available contextual crop; otherwise fail closed. Verify positive cue duration, source order, no remaining overlaps in the replacement or its neighboring joins, and deep-equal untouched captions. The original failed report remains immutable. Unrelated review flags must not be cleared.
6. **Failure stays honest.** Invalid/incomplete repair returns the original hypotheses with review flags; it does not make the export gate pass. Propagate recognition errors, abort, cancellation and timeout through existing job-failure semantics, as the parent proposes; do not swallow them as successful fallback. Retain the request ID and existing deadline. Do not emit block-complete progress/reply before repair finishes or extend liveness using synthetic progress.

**Limit:** retry agreement from the same model is additional context-conditioned evidence, not an independent truth oracle. The coverage association above remains an inference; actual source-grounded fixtures must challenge it. Where that association is uncertain, preserving non-equivalent text wins over obtaining clean subtitle timing. This design does not promise that the saved case will pass.
