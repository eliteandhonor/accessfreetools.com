## One Minimal Change
- Carry ONE unpublished whole-caption tail from block i into the ordinary transcription transaction for block i+1; reconcile before appending either that tail or the next block's boundary captions to completed state.
- Keep the existing 300-second blocks, five-second overlap, 30-second recognition windows, model pins/options, decoder, sample grid and within-block repair unchanged. Do not prefetch another block, add a model, extend right context, or repeatedly retry a boundary.
- This intentionally waits for the next normal block result, avoiding a new mid-block acknowledgement/resume protocol. It creates an opportunity for corroborated repair, not permission to force a match.

## Internal Contracts

type BoundaryTail = {

  consumedTailId: string | null;

## Tail And Repair Algorithm
1. After existing within-block repair, let E=block.end and B=nextStart=E-5. Seed the held suffix with captions affected by [B-5,E], including boundary point words; close over whole selected/raw captions and preserve append order. Retain PCM [max(block.start,E-30),E].
2. A held suffix or raw caption required by its closure extending left of that PCM bound makes this boundary ineligible: publish all original block results normally. Do not truncate captions/evidence or hold progressively older blocks. Record only a numeric/reason diagnostic.
3. Retain complete raw windows intersecting the retained PCM range, including point touches, plus completed guards in that range; preserve whole captions even outside the PCM as exclusion evidence, never as permission to infer outside it. Include any prefix entries displaced by taking an append-order suffix; if that breaks the bounds, bypass the optimization.
4. On block i+1, run its existing recognition/within-block repair once. Merge the saved provisional source with current results using existing source matching. Seed the cross-block repair ONLY from positive intersections between saved-tail and current-block captions; exclude solely within-block conflicts.
5. Form a bridge from saved PCM before B and current PCM from B through min(B+30,block.end): at most 55 seconds. Use absolute sample indices and one copy of the five-second overlap; no crossfade, time shift, sample duplication or interpolation change.
6. Reuse the existing repair planner/validator with cross-block seeds and an immutable-guard option. At most one contextual recognition per outer boundary attempt; whole-caption closure/merged seeds exceeding 30 seconds remain unresolved. Existing within-block call defaults must be unchanged.
7. Before inference, reject a rounded region touching ANY locked caption, exceeding retained PCM, omitting a selected/raw whole caption, or lacking alignment. Preserve the existing post-floor/ceil point-boundary recheck. Never discard inconvenient originals to fit the crop.
8. Apply only when unchanged `canReplaceWhisperHypotheses` accepts every original and all replacement words, with ordered positive-duration captions and no outside collision. Keep point/no-gap, ambiguity, lexical, CJK and punctuation restrictions unchanged; rejection/ordinary optional-retry error retains the original candidates.
9. Settle the old tail and current results together, then select at most one new tail at the CURRENT block's end. Old unresolved captions become completed with their review state; never accumulate a queue of unresolved PCM/tails.
10. At EOF, settle any incoming tail and append everything; return no pending tail. Short/insufficient final context fails closed without invented padding or timestamps.

## State, Stop And Edits
- `RUNNING(i,pending?) -> ACK(i+1,newPending?)`: after operation/receipt checks, compute `delta=mergeTranscriptSegments(sourceSegments,append).slice(sourceSegments.length)`; retain the actual canonical prefix and append delta to source and edited arrays. Assert prefix values/order unchanged; update both arrays, tail and nextBlock synchronously before rendering.
- Only completed entries receive public caption indices/keys; pending entries are not shown or exported while running. Never globally sort/reindex canonical state; retain existing presentation/export sorting.
- `STOP/ERROR -> SEALED_PARTIAL`: invalidate/terminate workers as today, then synchronously append saved pending.source to BOTH canonical arrays exactly once, without re-merging it against its own block. Keep one metadata-only SealedBoundary witness, clear pending/PCM, retain nextBlock, expose all acknowledged text for editing/download.
- Stop uses the authoritative checkpoint, not a stale render closure. The old async catch/finally may return after invalidation, so sealing must be in the Stop handler itself and in the current-operation error/control-error path. Receipt-before-Stop and Stop-before-receipt have deterministic, separately tested outcomes.
- `RESUME -> RUNNING(nextBlock)`: use the same File object, track and language checks and saved edited projection. A sealed tail is completed forever; resume may remove only positively proven incoming duplicates through the unchanged matcher, never replace its source/times/text or restore a user-deleted caption.
- The sealed witness is bounded in-memory provenance, not an active repair instruction; release it when that next block is acknowledged. A future separately tested exact-duplicate hook may consume it only to omit corresponding INCOMING occurrences, with whole-occurrence coverage and canonical source-index correspondence, never to rewrite completed or edited captions.
- `editSegment` updates the live checkpoint's edited projection and rendered state together; source evidence stays untouched. No fuzzy edit rebasing or text-keyed identity. On complete, normal editing remains available after checkpoint disposal.
- Pending survives ordinary WebGPU recycling and the single genuine GPU-to-WASM fallback because it belongs to the component, not the worker. Clone pending PCM into requests; transfer only disposable copies/current decoded audio, never the sole saved checkpoint buffer. Retry re-decodes the current block as today.
- Abort/stale/timeout never starts fallback or commits a late reply. Keep Lifecycle request IDs, idle/deadline limits and increasing-work progress checks unchanged; progress must not reach final completion before boundary settlement.
- Reset, replacement and unmount deliberately clear the checkpoint, witnesses and pending buffers with existing cleanup semantics; Stop/error preserve acknowledged text and witnesses. Unacknowledged in-flight block words still have the existing block-level recovery granularity; no crash/reload persistence is introduced.

## Bounds And Compatibility
- Extra retained PCM: one <=480000-sample Float32 tail (1.92 MB); bridge <=880000 samples (3.52 MB), retry view <=480000. No second five-minute PCM block or whole-recording audio retention. Use `slice`, never a tail view whose backing buffer pins 300 seconds.
- Cap each extra tail/guard/original or sealed-witness bundle at 128 captions, 2048 words and 32768 UTF-16 text units, including duplicated fields; retain at most one such bundle per checkpoint. Exceeding a cap publishes originals without this optimization; never truncate transcript output. Release old worker carry/bridge before constructing the next carry.
- Conservative additional live PCM budget, allowing main/worker old/new transfer copies plus bridge: <=11.20 MB; existing decoded block/model/output memory is separate. Actual JS GC, decoder and GPU peaks still need measurement. No retained transcript/media diagnostics, storage or telemetry.
- Strictly unchanged public behavior: NO. Normal running may show/export up to 30 seconds less text until the next block finishes, and the extra retry adds latency. Stop/error immediately exposes that saved text; controls, final export formats, privacy, limits and immutable completed/edit identities remain unchanged.
- Requiring immediate checkpoint publication AND later automatic correction of that same text is incompatible with immutable completed identities. A Stop-sealed boundary can remain unresolved; silently rewriting it is not an acceptable workaround. Broader success depends on real evidence, potentially the parent's separately tested provenance-aware corroboration, not on relaxing this design's gate.
