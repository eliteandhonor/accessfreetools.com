# Output System

## Job

Package SERPForge findings into clear artifacts: audits, briefs, keyword maps, topic clusters, technical recommendations, CTR rewrites, and growth roadmaps.

## Inputs

- Reports from `agents/serpforge-ai/reports/`.
- Workbench reports under `output/seo-agents/`.
- SEO review reports under `output/seo-tool-review/`.

## Output

- Short executive format for long reports: three wins, three issues, one best next action.
- Exact paths to evidence.
- Clear approval state.

## Guardrail

Separate facts, evidence, and recommendations. Never hide missing evidence behind confident wording.
